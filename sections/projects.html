Capstone Project - ResQMesh

Developed an emergency communication system designed to help the CDRRMO Valencia assist civilians during emergencies, especially when internet connectivity is unavailable. Integrated Orange Pi, LoRa modules, and GPS into mesh nodes to establish a decentralized communication network for distress signaling and emergency response monitoring.
